# Real Hologram Creation

This section is an introductory overview of physical holography, with links to detailed manufacturer instructions and course materials. Use the instructions for your particular recording material, laser, and processing kit when planning an experiment.

## DIY Laser Holograms with LitiHolo Kits

### Overview
LitiHolo kits use self-developing photopolymer plates, so no separate wet chemical processing is needed. The base Hologram Kit primarily makes transmission holograms; the Reflection Hologram Upgrade adds white-light-viewable reflection holograms and requires the base kit.

### Getting Started
1. **Choose a LitiHolo Kit**: Check the included plates and whether you need the reflection upgrade
2. **Set Up Your Workspace**: Use a stable surface and the kit's holography laser; an arbitrary laser pointer may not have the coherence and stability needed for recording
3. **Follow the Kit Instructions**: Each kit comes with step-by-step guidance
4. **Expose and Develop**: The film develops automatically with exposure

## INTEGRAF Reflection & Transmission Holograms

### Reflection Holograms
For reflection recording, the object and reference waves reach the recording layer from opposite sides. Suitable reflection holograms can be viewed with a small, directional white-light source at the appropriate angle; diffuse room lighting may not give a clear image.

**Setup Requirements:**
- Laser: a stable, sufficiently coherent source with a wavelength matched to the recording material, such as a suitable HeNe (632.8 nm) or diode laser
- Geometry: in a simple single-beam Denisyuk setup, the laser illuminates the object through the plate; no separate beam splitter is required
- Object-to-plate distance: follow the selected setup; INTEGRAF's beginner tutorial places the plate directly over the object
- Exposure: follow the material and kit instructions; time depends on illumination at the plate and the material's sensitivity

**Processing Steps:**
For chemically processed plates, use a developer matched to the emulsion. INTEGRAF's tutorial uses PFG-03M with JD-4, or PFG-01 with JD-2/JD-3. Follow the selected processing kit's complete sequence, including its specified rinses, bleaching or fixing, and drying steps. These wet-processing instructions do not apply to LitiHolo's self-developing photopolymer.

See [INTEGRAF's step-by-step reflection tutorial](https://www.integraf.com/resources/articles/a-how-to-make-holograms-step-by-step-tutorial) and [JD-4 processing instructions](https://www.integraf.com/resources/articles/a-how-develop-holograms-with-jd-4-kit).

### Transmission Holograms
Typical laser-transmission holograms are viewed with suitable laser illumination from behind the plate and can display full parallax and depth. White-light-viewable transmission holograms, such as rainbow holograms, use different recording arrangements.

**Setup Requirements:**
- Object and reference waves reach the recording layer from the same side
- Beam ratio: follow the selected material and setup; in a split-beam arrangement, the ratio at the plate is not simply the beam splitter's nominal ratio
- Stability: Critical - use vibration isolation

**Processing Steps:**
For chemically processed plates, follow the complete procedure for the selected emulsion and processing kit, as for reflection holograms. Fixing and bleaching are process-dependent, not universal steps for every transmission hologram. Self-developing photopolymer uses its manufacturer's dry recording procedure instead.

## Safety Considerations

### Laser Safety
- Always wear appropriate laser safety glasses
- Never look into the direct beam or its specular reflections
- Use beam stops and attenuators
- Follow the recording material's ambient-light or safelight requirements; room brightness is not a substitute for controlling laser exposure
- Post warning signs when laser is in use

See [OSHA's laser hazard overview](https://www.osha.gov/laser-hazards/hazards) and the laser manufacturer's safety instructions.

### Chemical Safety
- Work in well-ventilated areas
- Wear gloves and eye protection
- Follow MSDS (Material Safety Data Sheets) for all chemicals
- Properly dispose of chemical waste

## Troubleshooting

### Common Issues

**Weak or No Image**
- Check laser coherence length
- Verify proper exposure time
- Ensure beam ratio is correct
- Check for vibration during exposure

**Blurry Image**
- Improve vibration isolation
- Reduce exposure time if object moved
- Check laser stability
- Verify focus of reference beam

**No Reconstructed Image Under the Expected Viewing Conditions**
- Check the illumination wavelength and angle for the recorded hologram type
- Check beam alignment, laser coherence, and the recording beam ratio
- Review exposure and processing against the material's instructions before changing exposure time

The recorded interference fringes should not be expected to be visible to the unaided eye; assess the reconstructed image under suitable illumination.
